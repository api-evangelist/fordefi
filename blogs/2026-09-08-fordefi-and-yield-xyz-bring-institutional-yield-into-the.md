Fordefi customers can now discover, deposit into, and manage DeFi yield opportunities without ever leaving the platform. Fordefi, the MPC self-custody platform trusted by more than 350 institutions, has partnered with Yield.xyz to launch Earn. Earn is a new section of the Fordefi platform where trading firms, funds, and treasury teams can find institutional-grade yield opportunities, deposit into them, and manage every position from the same place they already secure their assets.
