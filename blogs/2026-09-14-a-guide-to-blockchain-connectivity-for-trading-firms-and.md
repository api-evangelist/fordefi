Institutions executing on-chain strategies need application access across many blockchains, a readable account of what each transaction will do, and approval controls that apply at the point of signing. This guide examines how browser extensions differ on all three and how to evaluate them. Why the Browser Extension Deserves a Closer Look Trading firms, asset managers, and funds increasingly execute strategies directly against on-chain protocols, including decentralized exchanges, lending markets, staking systems, bridges, and tokenized asset platforms.
