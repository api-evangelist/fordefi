Hypernative’s Transaction Guard now runs natively inside Fordefi. When someone on your team proposes a transaction in the Fordefi browser extension or mobile app, Hypernative screens it before it reaches an approver, and the findings show up right next to Fordefi’s own risk checks. Hypernative has been part of the Fordefi ecosystem for over a year as an external integration, and a number of joint customers already run it alongside their Fordefi workflow.
