Fordefi now supports the XRP Ledger. Institutions can hold and move XRP and Trust Line Assets from Fordefi vaults on XRPL Mainnet, with policy controls and MPC signing on every transfer. Trust Line Assets are the XRP Ledger’s issuer-based tokens, identified by a currency code and an issuing account.
