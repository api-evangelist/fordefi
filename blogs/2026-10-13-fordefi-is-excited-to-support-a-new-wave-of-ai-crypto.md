The newest idea in crypto is a blockchain that mines its coin by doing the math behind AI. Instead of the brute-force hashing that secures Bitcoin, miners run the same matrix multiplication that trains and serves AI models, and that work is what secures the network, at almost no cost beyond the AI computation itself. This is proof-of-useful-work, and it’s one of the more timely innovations we’ve seen reach crypto in a long time.
