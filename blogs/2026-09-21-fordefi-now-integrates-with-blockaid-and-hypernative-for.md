Fordefi now screens outgoing transactions with Blockaid and Hypernative before they are signed. Findings from your provider appear next to Fordefi’s own risk engine results in the transaction preview, the transaction details, and the transaction history, and each one is labeled with the provider that found it. The same results come back through the API, and admins can write policy rules that block, allow, or require approval based on what a provider found.
