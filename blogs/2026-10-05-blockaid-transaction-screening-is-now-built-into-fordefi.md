Blockaid’s transaction screening now runs natively inside Fordefi. When someone on your team proposes a transaction in the Fordefi browser extension or mobile app, Blockaid simulates and validates it before it reaches an approver, and the findings show up right next to Fordefi’s own risk checks. Fordefi and Blockaid first partnered in November 2025, when customers could add Blockaid’s Cosigner to their Fordefi workflow as a separate product.
